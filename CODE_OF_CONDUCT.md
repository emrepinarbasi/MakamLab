# Community conduct

MakamLab's project spaces are for respectful academic and technical
collaboration. Participants are expected to:

- discuss evidence and methods without personal attacks;
- acknowledge uncertainty and the limits of automated audio analysis;
- respect copyright, performer privacy, and research-ethics obligations;
- avoid discriminatory, harassing, threatening, or deliberately disruptive
  behaviour;
- report security problems privately rather than exploiting them publicly.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove material that violates these principles
and may restrict repeated abuse. Conduct concerns can be reported privately to
`emrepinarbasi@gmail.com`.
